Ventilator Preventive Maintenance Guide - Synthetic Training Document

Remove the ventilator from clinical use before service. Inspect the power cable, battery status, inlet filter, breathing circuit connectors, alarm speaker, display, and wheels. Replace a damaged cable rather than taping it. Run leak, pressure, flow, oxygen concentration, and alarm tests with approved test equipment.

After filter replacement, record the filter lot as synthetic training data and complete calibration. A failed battery self-test requires the unit to remain out of service until the battery is replaced and the full self-test passes.
