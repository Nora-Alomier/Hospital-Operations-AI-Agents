MRI Preventive Maintenance Guide - Synthetic Training Document

Before maintenance, establish the controlled MRI safety zone and verify that only MR-safe tools enter the magnet room. Inspect emergency stop controls, patient table travel, intercom audio, warning lights, coils, and visible cable damage. Record the cryogen indicator reading; only qualified personnel may service cryogenic components.

Quarterly work includes cleaning accessible air filters, checking table alignment, inspecting RF coils, and testing the intercom. Annual electrical and image-quality tests must follow the fictional manufacturer procedure. Cite this guide as the source when using these steps.
